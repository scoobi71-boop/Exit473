About Exit 473

Where Grenadian entrepreneurs, flavours, and culture come together in one extraordinary place.

Who We Are

More Than a Food & Lifestyle Park

Exit 473 is Grenada's premier multi-business destination — a curated collection of the island's finest restaurants, services, and cultural experiences, all under one roof in the heart of St. George's.

The name "Exit 473" is a nod to Grenada's international dialing code (+473), signalling to the world that this is the place to exit your routine and enter something extraordinary. We are the crossroads of flavour, creativity, and Grenadian community spirit.

From sizzling jerk grills and fine spice-infused tasting menus to precision barbershop cuts and live cultural performances — Exit 473 offers experiences that stay with you long after you leave the island.

Our Journey

The Story Behind Exit 473

Exit 473 was born from a simple vision: to create a place in Grenada where the best of everything comes together. A destination where locals are proud to bring their families and visitors are delighted to discover the true soul of the Isle of Spice.

The park brings together carefully selected businesses — each vetted for quality, authenticity, and commitment to service. Our tenants aren't just businesses; they're ambassadors of Grenadian culture and hospitality.

Located in St. George's, the capital and heartbeat of Grenada, Exit 473 is easily accessible and designed to welcome everyone — from the early morning café visitor to the late-night music lover.
